# Decisions — E01

- Agnolo's voice is the stock "Old Italian Man" library voice, directed to sound dry, muffled whenever he speaks from the next room.
- Scene 2's escalation: the Not Enough Time lute intro, then the sad-violin stinger, then a swell into the real chorus. Two new escalation cues were left out.
- "Make It With Me" is take 2, matching the overture's tempo and key. Takes 1 and 4 also have every word right.
- Agnolo's Ledger was cut into scene 6, right after "Then we will have learned something expensive" and before Leonardo's final address, so the $3,000-versus-$20 contrast lands just before he asks the listener for help.
- Money framing: contributions pay back Episode 001's artist time (30 days at $100 a day). They do not buy Episode 002, and they are not refundable.
- Scene 4: the line saying the needle funds the next transmission was cut; it contradicted that framing.
- Audio first. Visual styles come later; the video uses robot portraits with waveforms in their mouths.
